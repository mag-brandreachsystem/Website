Questions creators usually ask

What kind of creators do you work with?

Creators across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and podcasts who post consistently and have an engaged audience. Niche matters more to us than raw follower count.

Do I need a certain follower count to start?

No fixed minimum. What matters is an engaged, defined audience. We've closed deals for creators with under 20K followers in the right niche.

How does payment work?

You pay for the service directly. We don't take a cut of your brand deals. Pricing depends on your audience size and how much outreach volume you need.

How long until I see my first deal?

Most creators see their first brand response within 2–3 weeks of outreach starting. Timing depends on niche and how outreach-ready your content and audience data are.

Do I have to accept every deal you bring me?

No. You approve every offer before anything is signed. We run outreach and negotiation, but the final call is always yours.
